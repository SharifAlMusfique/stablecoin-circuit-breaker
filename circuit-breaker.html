RESPOND

Treasury policy

Rules the firm agreed in advance. When one fires, the app proposes the action; a named person approves it with a wallet signature. Nothing moves without a signature.

Rules

editable
R1
Amber warning

A coin is amber (or worse) for the hold time. Notify the risk manager and log an alert. No funds move.

R2
Red: propose a move

A coin is red for the hold time. Pause the simulation and propose moving part of that coin's holdings from TREASURY to SAFE. Needs a signed approval.

R3
No decision: escalate

Nobody approves or rejects in time. Log "escalated, no action taken". Nothing moves.

Changes apply from the next reading. Reloading the page restores the defaults (3 min, 5 min, 50%, 2 min). The approval window is in real minutes because the simulation is paused while it waits.

HONEST METHOD

How the rating works

STARTING ASSUMPTIONS
SignalHow it is calculatedGreenAmberRed
Price deviation |price − 1| × 100, in % < 0.3%0.3–1%> 1%
Volatility Standard deviation of the minute-to-minute price moves across the last 15 readings, compared with the coin's calm level. The 2 most extreme moves are left out, so a single spike does not count as volatility. < 2×2–4×> 4×
Supply change % change in circulating supply over the last 60 readings (one hour) < 1%1–3%> 3%
Overall rating = the worst signal

If any one warning light is red, the coin is red. Conservative, and easy to explain.

Time-in-colour rule

An alert is logged only when amber (or worse) holds for 3 simulated minutes (rule R1), or red holds for 5 (rule R2). Rule R2 then proposes a treasury move for approval.

One alert per episode

Each level alerts once. The episode ends when the coin has been green again for 60 simulated minutes (5 re-alerted on every wobble in the real March 2023 data).

Honest limits

Simulated data, one reading per simulated minute. It flags stress; it does not predict depegs. Thresholds would be calibrated against past events.
