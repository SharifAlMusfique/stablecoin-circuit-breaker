How the rating works

STARTING ASSUMPTIONS
SignalHow it is calculatedGreenAmberRed
Price deviation |price − 1| × 100, in % < 0.3%0.3–1%> 1%
Volatility Standard deviation of the minute-to-minute price moves across the last 15 readings, compared with the coin's calm level. The 2 most extreme moves are left out, so a single spike does not count as volatility. < 2×2–4×> 4×
Supply change % change in circulating supply over the last 60 readings (one hour) < 1%1–3%> 3%
Overall rating = the worst signal

If any one warning light is red, the coin is red. Conservative, and easy to explain.

Time-in-colour rule

An alert is logged only when amber (or worse) holds for 3 simulated minutes (rule R1), or red holds for 5 (rule R2).

One alert per episode

Each level alerts once. The episode ends when the coin has been green again for 5 simulated minutes.

Honest limits

Simulated data, one reading per simulated minute. It flags stress; it does not predict depegs. Thresholds would be calibrated against past events.
